Bio

I am a PhD student in Computational Sciences and Engineering at Koç University, advised by Mehmet Cengiz Onbaşlı. My research focuses on post-quantum cryptography and privacy-preserving machine learning, with additional work in secure multiparty computation and theoretical cryptography. During my MSc at Koç University, I worked with Prof. Alptekin Küpçü on security models, proofs, and implementations for distributed single-password authentication.
